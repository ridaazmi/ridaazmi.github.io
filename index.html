Executive Summary

Geospatial Data Scientist and Research Scientist with a PhD in Spatial Remote Sensing and 10+ years of hands-on experience in satellite image analysis, spatial Machine Learning, and complex geospatial data architectures. Strong expertise in developing automated analytical pipelines, spatial decision-support tools, and multi-source data integration for environmental and urban resilience. Author of 45+ peer-reviewed scientific publications and co-author of a book on spatial analysis in R.

Currently focusing on leveraging Geospatial Foundation Models (GFMs) for urban climate applications—combining Earth Observation (EO), multi-source geospatial data, and advanced AI architectures to better understand and model complex urban climate dynamics. My work explores the integration of foundation models with Agentic AI for automated scientific reasoning, tool orchestration, and scalable geospatial analysis, with a particular focus on urban heat, environmental dynamics, and climate-resilient cities.
